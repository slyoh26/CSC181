## 3. Preliminary Project Proposal

### Working project title

PounceBoard

### Problem statement

Due dates for quizzes, reports, and group projects are announced through multiple channels, such as MOLE, subject group chats, and in-class reminders, which may make it difficult for MSU-IIT students in a class section/block to stay updated. At the moment, block representatives communicate updates via message, and students rely on their own notes, screenshots, and memory. This could sometimes result in weeks when multiple demanding requirements or exams fall at the same time without anyone realizing it until the last minute, some missed submissions, cramming of activities resulting in a poor output across different subjects. Sudden changes in schedules also happen on a short notice. For example, a sudden shift to asynchronous or an open lab schedule will be notified to the members of the shared board. The team still needs to confirm how students currently track deadlines, and what MOLE does not already show. 

### Proposed solution

Our proposed solution is a shared tracker for deadlines, exams, and other important information that needs to be announced. An app that is like google calendar, tasks, and classroom combined. Block representatives create a section board, and add deadlines, exams, and announcements with the subject, type, and weight of each item. The members can confirm or report the announcement for any discrepancies. The app shows everything in a calendar and task view, so students can see what is due and mark items as done. It also shows a weekly workload view that flags weeks where several heavy requirements pile up, so students can plan earlier and officers can raise conflicts with instructors. Unlike MOLE, which only lists a single course’s activities, the board gathers dates from all of a section’s subjects, including those announced only in group chats, in one place.

## 5. Initial Scope

### Intended users and setting

The primary users/stakeholders will be the students and block representatives who will be using the platform daily to check the changes in their schedule, with the block representative having the additional use of being able to disseminate the information to their peers. They will be the most affected by the app and will benefit the most. Our second shareholders will be the professors, student councils, and society officers as they will be the users posting the various announcements on the platform. It is also possible for the block representatives to take on the role of posting the announcements for their class as admin within their block in case it was originally posted outside the platform. The setting and operation of the platform will be within the MSU-IIT academic environment and will be accessible on mobile and web based application used on smartphones and laptops during and outside of daily class operations

### In-scope capabilities

[Describe 3–5 specific capabilities as actions users can perform.]

1. Users can view a dynamic weekly schedule that updates with announcements that entail a change in schedule. This will make it easier to anticipate heavy workload periods and make it easier to spot free schedules during a particular class.
2. Users can create dedicated spaces for themselves. An example of this would be a block representative creating a space that only their blockmates can join. 
3. Block representatives can post announcements or deadlines with its subject, type (quiz, exam, report, group project) and a weight (light, medium, heavy). Block representatives may also assign additional members to post these deadlines when he’s not available.
4. Students may join a society depending on their college and announcements from that society will also be reflected in the app.

### Out-of-scope capabilities

[Identify at least three features, integrations, services, or settings
excluded from this semester's version.]

1. MOLE integration would be hard since the team would need to have permission to automatically pull deadlines from the website.
2. Grade tracking or grade calculation will not be part of this app as they are personal data.
3. Group chat or messaging features will not be included as this will just duplicate messenger.

### Expected deliverables

Working prototype of PounceBoard.
Demo data to test the app's features. Using fictional subjects, deadlines section/block members etc.
Source code in the team’s repository.
User documentation, a short guide for how to use the app.
Testing of the in-scope capabilities, plus a small usability test with classmates.
Final presentation of demo.

## 6. Review and Next Steps

### Change made after reviewing the proposal

- Before review: An assignment, project, quiz tracker.
- After review: Expanded into a shared block board featuring a weighted weekly workload view, calendar view, schedule change updates, and society-level announcement integration.
- Reason: Focusing solely on a simple deadline checklist overlaps too heavily with existing personal task apps. Adding a section-wide shared board with dynamic workload indicators directly solves the issue of overlapping heavy deadlines across subjects while giving block representatives a single point of truth.

### Biggest remaining risk

Data accuracy and adoption risk. Since automatic integration with MOLE and Google Classroom is currently out of scope due to limited access, the system relies entirely on block representatives and professors to manually input and maintain deadline data accurately and promptly.

### Three unanswered questions

1. What specific features or announcement types exist in group chats and in-class announcements that MOLE fails to capture or organize effectively?
2. How do instructors and block representatives currently handle sudden schedule changes (e.g., unexpected shifts to asynchronous classes or open lab times), and what is the fastest way for them to broadcast these updates in the app?
3. How will the app handle verification or moderation if a student submits a wrong or fake deadline entry on a shared block board?
